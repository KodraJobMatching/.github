## About KODRA

**KODRA is an accessibility-focused technology startup working to make employment more inclusive through AI-powered job matching, assistive technology integration, and workplace accessibility solutions.**

Traditional recruitment systems often overlook qualified candidates with disabilities because they focus on conventional job requirements without adequately considering individual capabilities, accessible work environments, or reasonable accommodations.

KODRA takes a different approach.

We connect candidates with opportunities by evaluating the relationship between their skills, functional capabilities, job demands, workplace conditions, and available accommodations.

Our goal is to help employers discover qualified talent while enabling candidates to explore meaningful employment opportunities without unnecessary barriers.

**We do not believe people should have to fit inaccessible systems. We believe technology should help make those systems accessible.**

## What We Offer

### 1. AI-Powered Job Matching

We aim to make recruitment more relevant, transparent, and inclusive by matching candidates with opportunities based on job-related criteria.

- Skills-based candidate-to-job matching.
- Comparison of job demands with individual capabilities and preferences.
- Consideration of workplace conditions and available accommodations.
- Explainable matching results with confidence indicators and contributing factors.
- Identification of potential accessibility gaps and opportunities to address them.

**Value:** Help candidates discover relevant opportunities and help employers identify talent beyond conventional recruitment filters.

### 2. Accessibility and Assistive Technology Integration

We design for different ways of accessing, navigating, and interacting with digital employment services.

Our accessibility approach includes support for technologies and interaction methods such as:

- Screen readers and keyboard navigation.
- Voice control and speech-based interaction.
- Eye-tracking and gaze-based interfaces.
- Switch access and alternative input devices.
- Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC).
- Adjustable text, contrast, spacing, and motion preferences.
- Simplified language, Easy-Read experiences, and multilingual interfaces.

**Value:** Make job discovery, profile management, and application workflows more accessible to people with diverse interaction needs.

### 3. Workplace Accessibility and Accommodation Solutions

We help structure the information needed to understand whether a job and its working environment can accommodate different capabilities.

- Workplace accessibility requirements.
- Physical and digital work environment considerations.
- Assistive technology and accommodation resources.
- Job-demand and functional-capability mapping.
- Potential workplace adjustments that may reduce accessibility barriers.

**Value:** Help employers evaluate accessibility requirements and explore practical ways to expand their talent pool.

### 4. Inclusive Recruitment for Employers

KODRA is designed to support employers in making recruitment more accessible without reducing candidates to disability labels.

- Structured job requirements and capability-based criteria.
- Accessibility-aware candidate discovery.
- Clear explanations of relevant matches.
- Visibility into job requirements and accommodation possibilities.
- Support for more consistent and inclusive recruitment workflows.

**Value:** Help organizations build more inclusive hiring processes and connect with qualified candidates whose capabilities align with their roles.

### 5. Privacy-Centered and Responsible AI

Accessibility should never come at the cost of dignity or privacy.

KODRA's design principles include:

- Candidate control over personal information and consent.
- Separation of identity information from matching workflows where appropriate.
- No diagnosis-based matching.
- No inference of disability from assistive technology preferences or interaction patterns.
- Explainable results and fairness evaluation.
- Human oversight of consequential recruitment decisions.
- No face, emotion, or voice-stress analysis for candidate evaluation.

**Value:** Build trust through responsible technology, transparent decisions, and privacy-conscious design.

## How KODRA Creates Value

KODRA approaches inclusive employment as a compatibility problem, not a diagnosis-based classification problem.

A meaningful match considers several dimensions:

- **Candidate:** Skills, experience, capabilities, and preferences.
- **Job:** Essential tasks, required skills, and working conditions.
- **Environment:** Physical accessibility, digital accessibility, and available support.
- **Accommodations:** Potential adjustments that can bridge the gap between job demands and individual capabilities.

Rather than automatically excluding a candidate when a mismatch appears, the platform is designed to help identify whether an appropriate accommodation could make the opportunity more accessible.

Our AI is intended to support informed decisions, not replace human judgment or determine a person's worth.

## Our Approach

### Accessibility by Design

Accessibility is incorporated into product architecture, interface design, development, and testing rather than treated as a final enhancement.

### Capability-Based Evaluation

We focus on job-related capabilities and requirements instead of using disability diagnoses as proxies for suitability.

### Responsible and Explainable AI

We aim to make matching outcomes understandable, evaluate potential bias, communicate uncertainty, and preserve appropriate human oversight.

### Privacy by Design

We prioritize data minimization, controlled information sharing, consent, and protection of sensitive candidate information.

### Inclusive User Experience

We support different ways of interacting with technology and aim to reduce unnecessary barriers throughout the employment journey.

## Technology and Innovation

KODRA combines modern software engineering with applied AI and accessibility research.

Our technical direction includes:

- **Frontend Engineering:** React, TypeScript, accessible component systems, and progressive web applications.
- **Backend Integration:** API-driven architecture and integration with .NET services.
- **AI and Machine Learning:** Job matching, explainability, evaluation, and fairness testing.
- **Accessibility Engineering:** Assistive technology compatibility, alternative input methods, and adaptive user interfaces.
- **Data Engineering:** Structured job requirements, capability profiles, accommodation resources, and data validation.
- **Quality and Testing:** Accessibility audits, usability testing, automated testing, privacy controls, and security validation.

We are developing the foundations for a platform in which accessibility, intelligent matching, and responsible data use work together.
